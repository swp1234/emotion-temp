# Emotion Temperature Choices

Ten everyday choices produce an author-created temperature metaphor. This is entertainment, not a psychological or health assessment.

## Contract

- Every option displays and adds 0–4 intensity points.
- Ten accepted answers total 0–40; `round(-10 + total / 40 * 50)` maps the total to −10–40°C and one of eight fixed ranges.
- No answer, total, temperature or result enters analytics or share copy.
- Seven funnel stages are exact-once: view, start, progress after five answers, complete, next click, successful share and related click.
- The result offers one private Stress Check action and two related reflections.
- Ad serving remains suspended while the invalid-traffic restriction is active.

Run `node scripts/verify-emotion-temp.js` from the portfolio root.
